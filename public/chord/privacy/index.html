Chord privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Chord is a music listening stats app for Android. It's built so that your listening history stays on your phone. This policy explains what Chord handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Chord has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your listening history is recorded, analysed and stored only on your phone.
  • The developer never receives your listening data.
  • The only network use is Google Play, for the optional Chord Pro purchase.

What Chord reads

Chord uses Android's notification access to see what supported music apps report as playing: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Amazon Music and similar apps. For each track it reads:

  • the title, artist and album,
  • the track length and how long you listened,
  • the cover art, when the music app provides it on the phone.

Chord ignores notifications from every other app, and does not read message contents or any other notification content.

What Chord stores, and where

Chord stores your plays (track details, when you listened and for how long) and the cover images in its private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it.

If you import a listening history file from a music service, Chord reads it on your phone, keeps only the plays it extracts, and then deletes its copy of the file. Nothing is uploaded.

When data leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only through Google or by your own action:

  • Chord Pro purchase. Purchases go through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Chord receives only whether you own Chord Pro. Google's Play Billing library, which Chord uses for this, sends diagnostic information about purchases to Google. Your listening data is never part of a purchase.
  • Android's automatic backup. If Google backup is on for your phone, Android includes Chord's play history in your phone's backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms. With a screen lock set, that backup is end-to-end encrypted. Cover images are not included. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • Backup files you make. Chord's Backups screen creates a file with your plays and cover images. It goes only where you choose to save or share it.
  • Feedback you send. Send feedback opens your email app with your phone model, Android version, Chord's version, and Chord's status (whether it's recording, plus counts of plays). It includes no titles, artists or other listening details. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Chord, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything Chord has stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere, and Android's backup in your Google account, are under your control and can be deleted there.

Children

Chord is not directed at children under 13.
